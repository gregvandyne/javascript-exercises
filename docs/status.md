# Status

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

## How to run
`npm install && npm run seed && npm start` → http://localhost:3000 (Node 22, no API key needed). Tests: `npm test` (unit + safety), `npm run test:e2e` (Playwright). Details in README.

## Not verified / known limits
- **The automatic Teacher plan trial hasn't run against real Stripe yet** (only the fake Stripe): `subscriptions.create` with `trial_period_days` and no payment method, and the at-once cancel when a school seat takes over. A password sign-up with no email can't be matched by the one-trial-per-email rule until an email is given. With `STRIPE_TAX=1` these trials have no address or automatic tax until the card is added (docs/billing-go-live.md 5b).
- **The first live game has not been timed with a real teacher and class.** The step counts are real; the seconds are a headless browser's (about 13 s typing at 150 ms a key). Accounts from the short sign-up have no email, so no password reset by email until they start a trial. **Owner to decide:** these accounts are staff with no email and no review (only the "teacher, 18 or older" box), and they can open full-size rooms, make student accounts and ask for AI question drafts; limit them until an email is added, or accept it (docs/decisions.md "After the review of the first live game").
- **AI voices have never called OpenAI:** no key and no network here, so the API details (URL, fields, models, voices, price) are from memory. Listen to each language on `/admin/voices` (zh-Hant should be Taiwanese Mandarin) before switching it on.
- **Parents and teachers may now sign up with Google** (personal Gmail or Workspace; owner decision 11 Nov 2026, the Privacy Policy says so; the lawyer's look is pending in docs/legal-review.md). Teachers who sign up with a password have no email until they give one on the school step or start a trial.
- **Sign in with Google hasn't run against real Google yet.** Under-18 students also need their school's Google admin to allow Oomba Academy (steps on /google-setup). A Google domain belongs to one school (a district with one domain for several schools needs us to change that). A legal review of school-directed Google sign-in (COPPA/GDPR) is recommended before a wide launch.
- **Google Classroom sync hasn't run against real Google yet.** Its Classroom scopes (two read, four optional write) are sensitive: Google must verify the app before teachers outside your test users can connect (docs/google-classroom.md). Students are matched by Google account only; a lesson's later due-date change isn't sent again. Students new to Oomba join on their first Google sign-in on the school's domain (no account at import); grades sent are completion grades. Legal review of what goes to Google is pending (docs/legal-review.md), and the /privacy text does not yet describe materials, topics, announcements or automatic grades.
- **The compliance page's legal positions** (COPPA consent model, FERPA school official, GDPR roles, DPA availability) are placeholders waiting for the owner and lawyer; the public page only invites schools to ask. The Terms still say "8 to 14" in two places.
- **The oomba.ai move hasn't run on the real domain yet** (DNS and Render's certificate are the owner's step). Home-screen apps installed from the old name keep opening it and get redirected; reinstall from oomba.ai. Builder "pro" switch, dismissed tips and preview size are per address and reset once.
