## Judgment calls

- Partial means the source confirms part of the row: Classroomscreen timers (jobs and check-ins not confirmed); Brainingcamp Share Codes send tasks to students (pausing screens not stated); Classroomscreen languages (the widget bar, work symbols and calendar change language, the rest of the app is English only, in their own words); Brainingcamp free option (a 30-day free trial, no free plan stated).
- Yes for a whole-class row when the source states it plainly: Classroomscreen games are on the shared screen ("ready-made games and templates"); "any device" counts for Chromebooks and phones.
- Kahoot! AI drafting: the quote is from a Kahoot!+ plan card on the schools page (a paid plan); the row says the feature exists, not that it is free.
