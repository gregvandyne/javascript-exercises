Who's playing?

  1. Roll and move. Land on a soccer player nobody owns? Sign him.
  2. Pass or land on Kick Off and collect $200.
  3. Land on someone else's player and you pay their fee. Own both players of one colour and the fee doubles.
  4. Championships work like railroads: the more trophies you own, the more everyone pays.
  5. Land on Shoot and a player takes a shot. If you own him, it's a goal and you get a check mark.
  6. Sign a goalkeeper 🧤 to block someone else's goal. After the save, he goes back to the bank.
  7. Roll doubles and you go again after your move.
  8. Land on the Red Card corner and you go straight to the Sin Bin. Pay $50, use a Ref's Pardon, or roll doubles to get out.
  9. On your turn, tap Trade to offer cash, players and trophies to another player.
  10. Run out of money? You must sell players and trophies back to the bank for half price, or auction them to the other players. Nothing left to sell means you're bankrupt and out!
  11. First to 3 check marks wins the league, or be the last player standing.
